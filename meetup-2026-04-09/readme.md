## 🎤 Sessions

---

### Session 1 — What I'll Tell My Kids About K8s Security
**Lars Lefebvre** · ING

A creative, story-driven talk that makes Kubernetes security approachable through metaphors and everyday analogies. In the second half, Lars shares the story behind an open source tool built at ING to help teams uncover and understand real-world security issues in their clusters.

> **Bio:** Lars is a Cloud and Application Security Engineer with a strong background in the Cloud Native stack, experienced in managing multi-cloud environments and enhancing security in software development lifecycles.

---

### Session 2 — K8s-wish and Other LLM Adventures
**Andrew Yourtchenko**

Can we use LLMs to avoid writing YAML? This talk explores two approaches — a single-shot LLM call and a full agentic loop — using two open source projects, and what each gets right and wrong.

> **Bio:** After 25 years at Cisco across TAC Security, IPv6 technical marketing, and high-performance networking software, Andrew now builds open source agentic AI tools. Co-author of DHCPv6 and other RFCs, holder of 4 US patents.

---

### Session 3 — Kubestronaut Panel & Q&A
**Olivier Calzi · François Duthilleul · Evelien Schellekens**

Meet a panel of Kubestronauts — some even Golden. After a quick intro to what a Kubestronaut actually is, we dive straight into an interactive Q&A: their journey, what the grind really looks like, and what they'd do differently.

> **Olivier Calzi** ⭐ *Golden Kubestronaut* — From pulling Ethernet cables as a student in France to 15 CNCF certifications, Olivier has spent 10 years learning the stack from the ground up. Now a Cloud Native Expert focused on SRE and infrastructure architecture, and a firm believer in lifelong learning.

> **François Duthilleul** ⭐ *Golden Kubestronaut* — Principal Solutions Architect at Red Hat, leading security in the EMEA Telco CoE. Co-author of the OpenShift Security book, first Belgian Golden Kubestronaut, 2025 Top SSA. Find him at rock concerts, on a road bike, or talking cloud-native over a craft beer.

> **Evelien Schellekens** 🌟 *Kubestronaut* — Principal Solutions Architect at Elastic. Passionate about observability, open source, and sharing knowledge through public speaking. Active contributor to the Kubernetes and OpenTelemetry communities.
